## HoodLynx

### The Chain
The invisible agreement that allowed civilization to function.
Every promise. Every trade. Every act of trust. Every alliance. Every sacrifice. Together they formed The Chain.

### The Severing
The Chain wasn't destroyed. It was Severed. Someone (or something) discovered how to manipulate trust itself.
People stopped believing. Kingdoms collapsed. Markets failed. Communities fractured. Truth became impossible to distinguish from deception. The Chain disappeared.

### Fragments
Fragments of Consensus. Every Fragment contains one undeniable truth. Not information. Truth. A Fragment cannot lie. It cannot be manipulated. Whoever possesses one can expose deception.
One Fragment proves ownership. Another proves identity. Another reveals intentions. Another exposes corruption. They remove uncertainty.

### Signal
Signal isn't emitted by a Fragment. A Signal happens. Whenever a Fragment is awakened. Whenever someone attempts to alter reality around a Fragment.
It explains why Signals suddenly appear. Someone has found one. Or someone is trying to corrupt one.

### Rose & Cross
A forgotten order. Long before the Severing. There existed an organization that understood what the Chain actually was. They predicted the collapse.
They hid records. Maps. Symbols. Methods. They left breadcrumbs everywhere. Only after the Severing did people realize. They were trying to warn everyone.

### The Ending?
Every recovered Fragment suddenly assembles. The community believes they're rebuilding the Chain. Instead They discover They were never rebuilding it.
They were rebuilding The Map. The map points to one final hidden location. The Mint on Gravemint.io
The mint isn't "buy NFTs." It's Activation. Every minted HoodLynx becomes. An officially recognized Hunter.
Not because they bought an NFT. Because they completed the investigation.

### The Hunters / HoodLynx
Instead of players trying to "find a code." They're unknowingly becoming. Hunters.
Every clue they solve. Is another Fragment recovered. Not literally. Narratively. The community is rebuilding the Chain together.
Return Fragments to the Chain and keep them out of anyone's control.
Believe the Chain belongs to everyone. Recover Links. Protect them. Steal from those abusing power.
Protect the Chain. The guardians. They recover Links before others can claim them.
"The Chain belongs to everyone."
Who should be trusted with this Link?

### The Seekers / Owls
Not a faction. An occupation. People who study Signals. They don't fight. They don't collect Fragments. They simply. Observe. Record. Guide.
It sees what others overlook. Now the Hunters constantly consult Seekers.
Preserve Fragments because history must not be forgotten, even if the Chain is never rebuilt.
They give the world wisdom. Without them, everyone is just fighting over the Links. The Keepers remind the audience that the Chain existed long before the current conflict, and someone has spent generations making sure its history wasn't forgotten.
"Some truths must survive, no matter who wins."
Why was this Link created?
They shouldn't simply "protect knowledge." They should be the custodians of the Chain's memory. That makes them feel ancient, respected, and mysterious.
The Keepers are the oldest surviving order within the Hood. They do not seek power. They do not seek control. They seek understanding.
Long before the factions emerged, they were entrusted with a single responsibility:
Every recovered Link is studied, documented, and preserved before it is ever wielded. They believe the greatest danger is not a Link falling into the wrong hands — it's the world forgetting why it existed in the first place.
The Pride often turns to the Keepers for guidance.
The Crown seeks their knowledge.
The Broken hunt them for the secrets they refuse to reveal.
Some say they know what truly caused the Severing.
Remember the Chain.
They preserve its history, study the Links, and safeguard knowledge that could change the world.

### The Crown / Crowns
The Crown wasn't always evil. Before the Severing. They protected civilization. Afterward.
They concluded humanity couldn't be trusted. Their belief, Freedom caused the Severing. Control prevents another one.
Reassemble the Chain under centralized rule to prevent another Severing.
Believe civilization needs order. The Links should belong to one authority. They're willing to sacrifice freedom for stability.
Control the Chain. They believe order requires centralized power.
How can this Link bring order?
"Order demands control."

### The Broken / Broken Chains
The Broken were Hunters. Keepers. Crown, Everyone. People exposed to too many Fragments.
The truth broke them. Now they believe. The Chain deserved to fall.
Believe the Severing was liberation. They don't want to rebuild anything, they want to corrupt or destroy Fragments so no one can ever restore what was lost.
Former Hood Lynx. They abandoned the Code. Some sought power. Some were corrupted by the Links themselves. This naturally explains Skeletons, Zombies, scarred variants
