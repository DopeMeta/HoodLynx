# Art prompt — Crown reveal / reclaim setup

For the Fragment 003 Crown discovery post. Visual only. No post copy on the image.

## Prompt

```text
Clean cinematic cyber-noir epic still for HoodLynx. Sharp, high resolution, minimal grain, neon lime-green and black with muted gold accents.

Foreground / midground:
Anthropomorphic hooded lynx Hunters in a ruined overlook or industrial plaza, studying Crown residue and evidence on the ground and walls: broken Signal glitch marks, gold seal stamps, scattered intercept fragments, faint chain-link Fragment traces. They look ready to move out and reclaim what was taken. Tactical, tense, determined. Not celebrating.

Technology:
A few small Hunter drones / recon drones hovering near them, scanning the residue and projecting faint green Signal traces toward the distance. Tech should feel useful and grounded, not sci-fi clutter.

Background:
Distant and massive, a towering royal feline Crown statue or monument in ornate dark armor and a literal crown, low-angle and ominous, same vibe as the attached reference. Around the city behind it: Crown banners and flags with simple three-point crown symbols. Surveillance towers, antennae, green monitor lights. The Crown presence feels everywhere, but the Hunters are the emotional focus.

Story read without text:
Hunters found the evidence. The Crown is real. The reclaim is about to start. A Fragment / chain-link object may be hinted far away near the statue, small and unclear, not a crystal.

Do NOT include:
post captions, HUD paragraphs, "ORDER DEMANDS CONTROL", "THE CROWN", terminal UI, numbers, wallet forms, busy collage walls of readable text.

Optional tiny HoodLynx mark in a corner only.
Mood: epic reveal through environment, hunters primed to retrieve.
Style: premium clear digital cinematic poster.
Use the attached statue image as style/reference for the distant Crown monument only.
```

## Negatives

```text
grain, noise, blurry, muddy, HUD text blocks, slogans, terminal UI, crystal gem fragment, purple, cute, comedy, watermark, crown statue as tiny sticker, empty evidence scene with no hunters
```
