## 2) Why the Broken are here (fun + necessary)

**Short lore:**
Activation does not filter for purity. When the four Fragments formed the Path, Broken corruption was already in 004. Contained, not erased. When the portal opened, ruin walked through with everyone else.

**Why holders should want Broken:**
- Tragic power, not joke evil — “they were us once”
- Chaos agency: glitches, sales, raids, bad endings if they dominate
- Visual flex (skeleton / corruption)
- Creed heat: **THE CHAIN DESERVED TO FALL**

**Story engines:**
1. **Contamination beats** — random Broken Access windows (sales, trait flips, mini-events)
2. **Purge attempts** — Hunters/Seekers try to cleanse; never fully works
3. **Broken councils** — holder votes that sabotage or accelerate story beats
4. **Origin crumbs** — weekly Archive pages: what broke them, who they were

## 3) Why Crown traits are common across the collection

Crown shouldn’t only mean “Crown faction NFT.” Crown is **pressure that marked everyone.**

**Best explanation (use this):**
After the Severing, The Crown occupied the ruins of trust. Their marks — crowns, gold order, control iconography — got stamped onto gear, canisters, and survivors as compliance, spoils, or infiltration. When Activation pulled every force through the Path, **Crown residue came with the bodies**, even on Hunters, Seekers, and Broken.

So:
- **Crown type / heavy Crown loadout** = loyalists / thralls of Order
- **Crown traits on non-Crown** = occupation scars, stolen regalia, forced badges, trophies from reclaim wars
- Common Crown traits = “the throne touched almost everything” — not a trait bug, a world fact

**Fun line for posts:**
> The Crown doesn’t need every Lynx to serve.  
> They only needed their mark to survive the Path.
