# The Crown

Also referenced as: **Crowns**

## Origin

The Crown was not always antagonistic.

Before the Severing, they protected civilization.

Afterward, they concluded humanity could not be trusted.

## Belief

> Freedom caused the Severing. Control prevents another one.

> "Order demands control."

## Mission

Reassemble the Chain under centralized rule to prevent another Severing.

- Civilization needs order
- Links should belong to one authority
- Willing to sacrifice freedom for stability
- Control the Chain

## Guiding question

How can this Link bring order?
