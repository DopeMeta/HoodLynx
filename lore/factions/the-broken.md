# The Broken

Also referenced as: **Broken Chains**

## Origin

The Broken were once Hunters, Keepers, Crown — everyone.

They were people exposed to too many Fragments. The truth broke them.

## Belief

The Chain deserved to fall.

The Severing was liberation.

> "Power belongs to those willing to take it."

## Mission

They do not want to rebuild anything.

They want to **corrupt or destroy Fragments** so no one can ever restore what was lost.
