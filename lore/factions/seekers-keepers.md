## Shared core (safe to use)

This group is not primarily a war faction. They are the memory / observation layer around the Chain.

### If using Seeker / Owl framing

- Not a faction — an **occupation**
- Study Signals
- Do not fight
- Do not collect Fragments
- Observe, record, guide
- See what others overlook
- Hunters consult them

### If using Keeper framing

- Custodians of the Chain's memory
- Oldest surviving order within **the Hood** (Hood undefined)
- Do not seek power or control — seek understanding
- Study, document, and preserve every recovered Link before it is wielded
- Greatest danger: the world forgetting why a Link existed
- Creed: remember the Chain; preserve history; safeguard knowledge that could change the world

## Beliefs / lines

- Preserve Fragments because history must not be forgotten — even if the Chain is never rebuilt
- "Some truths must survive, no matter who wins."
- Guiding question: **Why was this Link created?**
