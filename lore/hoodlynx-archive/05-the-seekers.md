## Designation

**Seekers.**

Early field notes used other names. Some said Keepers. Some said Readers. The Archive settled on Seekers because that is what the work actually is: seeking meaning under pressure, not guarding a finished book.

Public transmissions prefer this name. Use it.

---

## Function

Seekers do not recover canisters.

Seekers decrypt.

They sit with recovered text until it stops being noise. They argue. They annotate. They refuse the first explanation that sounds good. They publish only when the reading can survive daylight.

A Seeker without material is a theorist. A Seeker with Fragments is a threat to whoever preferred silence.

---

## Relationship to Hunters

Hunters bring the object home.

Seekers open what the object carries.

Neither role completes the other alone. Recovery without interpretation is trophy collecting. Interpretation without recovery is fiction with confidence.

The Archive exists because both roles kept working.

---

## Method

Seeker work tends to look like this:

1. Receive a recovery report.
2. Isolate absolute text from rumor.
3. Cross-check against prior Fragments.
4. Separate what the text says from what the faction wants it to say.
5. Publish a reading that can be attacked.

Attack is welcome. Unchallenged readings rot.

---

## Known Bias

Seekers want coherence.

That desire is useful and dangerous. Coherence can reveal structure. Coherence can also invent a story the Fragments never authorized.

The safest Seeker posture is humility with teeth: demand evidence, then refuse to worship your own conclusion.

---

## Standing and Access

In the present crisis, Standing is a measure of who still has access when channels tighten. Seekers without Standing still interpret. Seekers with Standing interpret closer to the source.

Do not confuse access with correctness.

---

## Archive Rule

When a Seeker publishes, mark:

- absolute text
- provisional reading
- contested reading

Never let those three share a single unmarked paragraph.

---

## Closing Note

The Seekers are not priests of the old Chain.

They are the reason the Signal became language again.
