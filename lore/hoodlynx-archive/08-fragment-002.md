## Archive Entry 002

> Fragments are remnants of the Chain.  
> Each carries a memory from before the Severing.  
> Alone they reveal little.  
> Together they reveal the truth.

This is ontology with teeth.

Not trophies. Not faction banners first. Remnants. Memory-bearing remnants. Thin alone. Dangerous together.

That is why stacking matters.  
That is why rival custody matters.  
That is why one seized Fragment can stall an entire reading.

Read beside 001: the Severing was chosen, and Fragments still carry what came before. Early Seekers heard “together they reveal the truth” as a promise the old Chain would return. Natural hope. Incomplete hope.

Entry 003 later named Path instead of restoration. Entry 002 never promised nostalgia. It only promised that truth shows up in assembly.

Every recovery is political.  
Who holds the remnant shapes which truth gets spoken in public.

Alone they reveal little.  
That line is also a warning about reading one page and declaring the war over.
