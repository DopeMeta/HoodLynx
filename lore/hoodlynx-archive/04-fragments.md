# Fragments

**HoodLynx Archive · Record 04**  
**Status:** ACTIVE

---

Fragments are remnants of the Chain.

That is not poetry. That is Archive Entry 002:

> Fragments are remnants of the Chain.  
> Each carries a memory from before the Severing.  
> Alone they reveal little.  
> Together they reveal the truth.

A Fragment does not argue. It does not care who arrives first. In containment it looks like a chain-link remnant in a canister. In decryption it becomes an absolute line the world has to swallow.

Hunters recover them.  
Seekers record them.  
Crown tries to seize them.  
Broken try to ruin them.

Alone, a Fragment is a spark.  
Together, Fragments form pattern.

Early hope said enough sparks would rebuild the old circle. Fragment 003 challenged that hard. Fragment 004 may decide whether “completion” means restoration… or something else.

Handle every Fragment as truth under pressure.  
Truth under pressure burns.
