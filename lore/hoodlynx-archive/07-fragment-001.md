## Recovery Context

Fragment 001 was the first recovery the public could watch decrypt in real time. Authorization windows opened around it. Hunters raced the Signal. Seekers waited for the line that would survive daylight.

The caption said the Archive had revealed its first truth. The truth itself arrived as Archive Entry 001.

---

## Absolute Text — Archive Entry 001

> THE SEVERING WAS NOT AN ACCIDENT. SOMEONE MADE THE CHOICE.

Visual pairing in early recoveries: a broken circular chain.

---

## Surface Reading

The wound had an author.

Accident language softens catastrophe into weather. Entry 001 refuses weather. It names intention. Someone chose the cut. That someone had motive, method, and enough access to make the Chain fail where it mattered.

For newcomers, this is the first hard door: the present crisis is not natural decay. It is aftermath.

---

## Deeper Reading

“Someone” is deliberately incomplete.

The Archive does not yet assign a proper name to the hand that chose. That absence is not Seeker laziness. It is discipline. Naming without proof creates a new lie with better branding.

What Entry 001 does force:

1. The Severing is historical, not mythical.
2. Agency existed before the fall.
3. Memory of that agency survived inside Fragment form.

If someone chose once, someone can choose again — to restore, to seize, to finish the ruin, or to build a Path the old Chain never authorized.

Later factions make more sense once this line is accepted. Crown seeks control over what choice remains. Broken seem obsessed with the justice or necessity of the fall. Hunters recover evidence. Seekers refuse to let “accident” return as comfort.

---

## Contested Points

- Was the chooser singular, collective, or institutional?
- Was the choice mercy, coup, sabotage, or sacrifice?
- Did the Signal preserve this line as accusation, warning, or invitation to investigate?

No sealed consensus. Productive disagreement continues.

---

## Narrative Function

Fragment 001 teaches the grammar of the hunt:

Something broke on purpose. Something still speaks. Recovery is possible. Meaning is not automatic.

Without 001, later Fragments look like random drops. With 001, they look like a memory system trying to reassemble under hostile conditions.

---

## Closing Note

Fragment 001 does not show the Chain restored.

It shows that forgetting was never innocent.
