# Fragments

## Canon

Fragments are **Fragments of Consensus**.

Each Fragment contains one undeniable truth.

Not information. **Truth.**

## Properties

- A Fragment cannot lie
- A Fragment cannot be manipulated
- Whoever possesses one can expose deception
- Fragments remove uncertainty

## Examples of what Fragments prove

- ownership
- identity
- intentions
- corruption

## Narrative role

Every clue the community solves is another Fragment recovered — not always literally, but narratively. Recovery brings the Archive / truth closer.
