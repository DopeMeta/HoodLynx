# Fragments

## Canon

Fragments are **Fragments of Consensus**.

Each Fragment contains one undeniable truth.

Not information. **Truth.**

## Properties

- A Fragment cannot lie
- A Fragment cannot be manipulated
- Whoever possesses one can expose deception
- Fragments remove uncertainty

## Examples of what Fragments prove

- ownership
- identity
- intentions
- corruption

## Public archive wording (ARCHIVE ENTRY 002)

> Fragments are remnants of the Chain.  
> Each carries a memory from before the Severing.  
> Alone they reveal little.  
> Together they reveal the truth.

## Narrative role

Every clue the community solves is another Fragment recovered — not always literally, but narratively. Recovery brings the Archive / truth closer.

## Related term: Link

Faction writing often says **Link** instead of Fragment (e.g. "who should be trusted with this Link?").

Treat Link as **possibly synonymous** until decided. See `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.
