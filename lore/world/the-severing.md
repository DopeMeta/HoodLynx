# The Severing

## Canon

The Chain was not destroyed. It was **Severed**.

Someone — or something — discovered how to manipulate trust itself.

## Consequences

- People stopped believing
- Kingdoms collapsed
- Markets failed
- Communities fractured
- Truth became impossible to distinguish from deception
- The Chain disappeared
