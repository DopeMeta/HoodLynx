# Rose & Cross

## Canon

A forgotten order that existed **long before the Severing**.

They understood what the Chain actually was. They predicted the collapse.

## What they left

- records
- maps
- symbols
- methods
- breadcrumbs everywhere

## Realization

Only after the Severing did people understand: Rose & Cross had been trying to warn everyone.

## Story function

Source of hidden trail material — symbols, maps, buried instructions — that Hunters can rediscover.
