## Naming call: Broken vs Corrupted

**Recommend public faction name: The Broken.**

| Option | Take |
|--------|------|
| **The Broken** | Best fit. Echoes Broken Chain, Severing, trust collapse. Feels like a people who were *shattered by truth*, not a disease label. Matches existing necklace trait + skeleton/zombie visual language. |
| **The Corrupted** | Weaker as a faction name. Generic fantasy/crypto. Better as a **verb/adjective** (“corrupted by Fragments”) than the title of the group. |
| **Broken Chains** | Poetic alias / poster line. Too long as everyday faction name. |

Use: **The Broken** in posts and traits. Keep “corrupted / corruption” as flavor in art notes, not the brand.
