## Critical correction: 4 / 66 / 3

Posters like “4 Hunters…”, “66 Days…”, “3rd Fragment…” were primarily a **Terminal code puzzle** spelling **4663**, plus flavor that Crown opposes Hunters.

They were **not** meant to hard-lock “Crown kidnapped 4 Hunters” as central lore.
